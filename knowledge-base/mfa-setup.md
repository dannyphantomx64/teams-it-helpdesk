# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup

## What is MFA?

Multi-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. After entering your password, you will need to verify your identity through a second method.

## Setting Up Microsoft Authenticator (Recommended)

1. Download **Microsoft Authenticator** on your phone (App Store or Google Play)
2. Go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo on your computer
3. Sign in with your company account
4. Click **+ Add sign-in method**
5. Select **Authenticator app** and click **Add**
6. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone
7. Tap **+** then **Work or school account**
8. Scan the QR code shown on your computer screen
9. Approve the test notification on your phone
10. Click **Done**

## Alternative MFA Methods

### Phone Call
1. Go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo
2. Click **+ Add sign-in method**
3. Select **Phone** and enter your mobile number
4. Choose **Call me** or **Text me a code**
5. Verify with the code or call

### Hardware Token
Contact IT Support to request a hardware FIDO2 security key. These are recommended for high-security roles.

## Common MFA Issues

- **I did not receive the notification**: Open the Authenticator app manually and check for pending approvals. Ensure push notifications are enabled.
- **My Authenticator app codes do not work**: Make sure your phone time is set to automatic. Incorrect time causes code mismatches.
- **I lost my phone**: Contact IT Support immediately at ext. 4357 to temporarily disable MFA and set up new methods.
- **MFA prompt not appearing**: Clear browser cache, try incognito mode, or use a different browser.

## Security Reminders

- Never share MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff
- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not initiate
- Report suspicious MFA prompts to security@company.com immediately
