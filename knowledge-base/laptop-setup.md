# New Laptop Setup Guide

## Day One Setup

### Step 1: Power On and Sign In

1. Open your laptop and power it on
2. Follow the Windows or Mac setup wizard
3. When prompted, sign in with your company email address
4. Enter your temporary password (provided by IT during onboarding)
5. You will be prompted to create a new password

### Step 2: Connect to Wi-Fi

1. Connect to **Corp-Secure** (see Wi-Fi guide for details)
2. Enter your company credentials when prompted

### Step 3: Install Required Software

1. Open **Company Portal** (pre-installed)
2. Install the following required apps:
   - Microsoft Office 365
   - GlobalProtect VPN
   - Microsoft Teams
   - Company antivirus (auto-deployed)
3. Restart your laptop after installations

### Step 4: Set Up Email

1. Open Microsoft Outlook
2. Your company email should auto-configure
3. If not, add account manually with your company email address and the server outlook.office365.com

### Step 5: Set Up MFA

Follow the MFA setup guide to configure multi-factor authentication on your account.

### Step 6: Enroll in Device Management

1. Open Settings then Accounts then Access work or school
2. Click **Connect** and sign in with your company account
3. This enrolls your device in Intune for security compliance

## Hardware Issues

### Laptop Will Not Start

1. Ensure it is charged. Plug in the charger and wait 5 minutes.
2. Try a hard reset: hold the power button for 15 seconds
3. If the screen remains black, try connecting to an external monitor
4. Contact IT if none of the above works

### Docking Station Not Working

1. Check all cable connections
2. Unplug the dock power and replug after 10 seconds
3. Try connecting your laptop directly to the monitor
4. Update display drivers through Company Portal

### Keyboard or Trackpad Issues

1. Restart your laptop
2. Check for driver updates in Settings then Windows Update
3. Try an external keyboard or mouse as a temporary fix
4. Submit a hardware repair request to IT
